# Tahoe resort logos

These marks identify the resorts visited; they do not imply sponsorship or affiliation. All logos remain the property of their respective owners.

- Northstar, Heavenly, and Kirkwood: vector paths exported from the official Vail Resorts [SVG icon font](https://www.northstarcalifornia.com/Assets/fonts/icons.svg), using the `resorts-logo-northstar`, `resorts-logo-heavenly`, and `resorts-logo-kirkwood` glyphs used by the resort website headers. Font coordinates are flipped to SVG coordinates and tightly framed; no paths are redrawn.
- Palisades Tahoe: original [official SVG mark](https://www.palisadestahoe.com/-/media/palisades-tahoe/footer/logos/pt-mobile.svg), linked in the [resort homepage](https://www.palisadestahoe.com/).
- Sierra-at-Tahoe: original [official SVG logo](https://sierraattahoe.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/sierra-logo.svg), linked in the [resort homepage](https://sierraattahoe.com/).

Retrieved October 9, 2026. Stored locally so the map does not depend on third-party image requests.
